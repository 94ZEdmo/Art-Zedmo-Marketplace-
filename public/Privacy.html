Privacy Policy - ART-ZEDMO Marketplace

Last updated: November 26, 2026 | Politique de Confidentialité - Dernière mise à jour : 26 Novembre 2026

1. Who we are / Qui sommes-nous

ART-ZEDMO Marketplace is a marketplace for African art based in Benin. It allows artists to publish artworks, collectors to discover, vote, and buy artworks using Pi, USDT, MTN MoMo and Moov Money.

ART-ZEDMO Marketplace est une plateforme béninoise de valorisation de l'art africain. Elle permet aux artistes de publier leurs oeuvres, aux collectionneurs de découvrir, voter et acheter avec Pi, USDT, MTN MoMo et Moov Money.

2. Data we collect / Données collectées

  • Account data: Name, artist name, WhatsApp number, email, city, password (encrypted). / Nom, nom d'artiste, WhatsApp, email, ville, mot de passe crypté.
  • Pi Network data: When you log in with Pi, we receive your Pi username and Pi UID provided by Pi SDK to authenticate you. We do NOT receive your Pi private key or Pi password. / Lors de la connexion Pi, nous recevons votre nom d'utilisateur Pi et UID Pi fournis par Pi SDK. Nous ne recevons jamais votre clé privée ou mot de passe Pi.
  • Artworks: Title, description, price, images you upload.
  • Votes, exhibitions, auctions: Your votes and participations.
  • Payments: We do NOT store your bank card or Pi wallet secret. Pi payments are processed via Pi Network. MTN/Moov/USDT payments are verified manually via transaction reference you provide.
  • Technical data: Device, browser, IP address for security and fraud prevention.

3. How we use your data / Utilisation

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your artworks in the gallery
  • To process votes, exhibitions and auctions
  • To verify Pi payments and deliveries
  • To improve the app and prevent fraud
  • To contact you via WhatsApp for orders / support

We never sell your personal data.

4. AI Assistant / Assistant IA

Our AI chatbot helps you discover artworks. Your messages to the chatbot are sent to our AI provider (Groq) to generate a response. We do not use your chatbot messages for advertising.

5. Sharing / Partage

Your public artworks, artist name and city are visible to all users. Your WhatsApp, email and password are NEVER public. We share data only with: Pi Network (for login/payment), our hosting provider (Render / MongoDB) for technical operation, and authorities if required by law.

6. Your rights / Vos droits

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your account and artworks by contacting us on WhatsApp. You can disconnect your Pi account at any time in Pi Browser.

7. Security / Sécurité

Passwords are hashed. Access to admin panel is restricted. Pi payments use Pi SDK official flow.

8. Children / Enfants

ART-ZEDMO is for users 18 years or older.

9. Changes / Modifications

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
